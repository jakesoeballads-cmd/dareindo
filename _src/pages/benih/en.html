Benih

A meeting place for people who work for the environment, from forests and oceans to balanced urban living. Benih means “seed”.

The problem

Many people and communities are already taking action for the environment. Unfortunately, they often still act on their own.

Beach clean-ups, tree planting and education events often never reach the people who would like to join. Businesses that want to get involved struggle to find the right community.

What Benih will offer

  • Community profilesEvery community and practitioner gets a page to introduce what they do.
  • Create and join activitiesAnnounce your activity, or find one you can join.
  • CategoriesForests, oceans, waste, urban living and more, so it is easy to find what interests you.

How it works

  1. Sign upAs an individual, a community or a business.
  2. Find or create an activityBy location and category.
  3. ConnectTake action together with others.
