The economy is no longer just numbers.

PT Daya Reforestasi Indonesia is an entity focused on developing an ecology-based economy. Its main concerns are restoring ecosystems, raising public awareness, and creating economic opportunities that keep the continuity and preservation of the environment in view.

1

Daya does not see environmental problems in isolation. Environmental damage is tied to how people use natural resources, how development is carried out, how communities meet their economic needs, and how awareness of and access to environmental knowledge are built.

2

That is why Daya is not here only to plant trees or bring back green cover. That work is part of a bigger idea: rebuilding a healthier, more harmonious relationship between people and the nature around them.

3

Daya sees itself as one of many ecology-based businesses. We do not claim to be the only ones with answers. We act as a driver, facilitator and space for collaboration where the public, communities, experts, enthusiasts, institutions and many others can contribute to the environment.

4

People and Nature

Nature is not just a resource, it is what makes life possible.

The philosophy of Daya Reforestasi Indonesia starts from a simple realisation: everything people need comes from nature.

5

Food, water, air, energy, building materials and so much else come from natural systems. Yet in the course of development, people often act as if they stood outside nature and had the right to rule over it.

6

Daya sees people as fundamentally part of nature. Our ability to build, develop technology and change our surroundings is a great power (in Indonesian, daya). But that power can also become a source of destruction when it is used without limits and without regard for the other inhabitants of nature.

7

So caring for the environment is not a “favour” people do for nature. It is part of our responsibility as one of nature’s inhabitants.

8

For Daya, sustainability does not mean that people stop using nature. People will always need nature. What has to change is how we take, process and use its resources, responsibly enough that its ecological functions can carry on.

9

People, Nature and the Economy

Daya does not see the environment and the economy as two interests that must always be set against each other.

On one hand, people’s economic needs are a reality that cannot be ignored. On the other, uncontrolled exploitation of nature can in the end destroy the very sources of life that the economy itself depends on.

10

That is why Daya wants to open up room for an ecology-based economy: an approach that lets economic activity go ahead while still taking into account people, soil, native plants, wildlife, water and air as parts of one balanced ecosystem.

11

With this approach, a programme’s success cannot be measured simply by how many trees were planted. Daya wants to look at the ecosystem as a whole: how the environment changes, how nearby communities benefit, how economic activity grows, and how public awareness shifts.

12

So Daya sees ecological, social and economic impact as different sides of the same success, and all of them need to be measured.

13

Core Belief

There is no economy in a damaged environment.

Daya believes that environmental damage in the end does not only create ecological problems, but also scarcity of resources and structural poverty.

14

The human economy needs soil, water, air, biodiversity and many other natural resources. When these systems are damaged, the foundations of the human economy weaken too.

15

So caring for the environment is not only a matter of conservation. It is part of keeping human life and the human economy possible for the long term.

16

Direction and Purpose

To build and grow an ecosystem of conservation-based businesses in which people, nature and economic activity can carry on and develop in harmony.

17

Daya exists to increase the number of businesses and economic actors that make environmental conservation part of how they earn a living.

18

Daya wants to create a collective power by bringing together as many people as possible who have the care, skills, knowledge, capital or opportunity to contribute to a more sustainable Indonesia, where a healthy environment goes hand in hand with people’s wellbeing and responsible economic activity.

Planting trees turns out not to be the hardest part.

Restoring degraded land involves many parties and many obstacles. Ironically, the obstacle we meet most often is the closest one: the people whose livelihoods already depend on that land.

Slopes that should be protected are planted with seasonal crops. Native plants that hold the soil together are replaced with shallow-rooted ones. This is not because people do not care. Families need to eat today, while the benefits of a forest take years to arrive.

Banning without offering alternatives only moves the problem elsewhere.

As long as protecting forests is seen as the opposite of earning a living, degraded land will keep growing.

Daya Reforestasi Indonesia is here to contribute to one simple aim: that caring for nature and sustaining the economy can move in the same direction, side by side.

Daya Reforestasi Indonesia is an entity working in the socio-ecological field. We chose to register as a company because restoration that can sustain itself lasts longer than a programme that only runs while it waits for the next round of aid.

We call it an ecosystem-based economy, in which a restored forest also becomes a fair source of income for the people around it.

  • Local people at the centre

    Local residents work on the project, guided by experts, and share in what the forest produces.

  • Growing as the land recovers

    Our business grows as the land recovers. You can check where our profit goes in our books.

Projects in development.

Turning bare land, especially on landslide-prone hillsides, back into a productive forest. Bamboo absorbs and holds water and helps bind the soil on slopes. Katuk and vanilla* provide income, and local native plants restore the ecosystem.

*Cash crops (other than bamboo) are chosen to suit the natural conditions of each site and the needs of the people nearby.

Transparency is the signature of Daya Reforestasi Indonesia

Structurally, Daya Reforestasi Indonesia combines its own ecosystem monitoring with financial transparency. This makes sure every investment is used efficiently and effectively, with a direct effect on environmental targets and a sustainable return.

We apply the same principle to every project we run: all budgets, operating costs and management income are open to investors and the public, backed by regular ecosystem monitoring.
