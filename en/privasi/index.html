Privacy Policy

Your data, your rights.

This policy explains the personal data we receive through dareindo.com, what we use it for, and your rights under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022, "PDP Law"). In effect from 19 November 2026.

Who we are

The data controller is PT Daya Reforestasi Indonesia ("Dareindo"). For any question or request about personal data, write to contribute@dareindo.com. Our registered address will be published on the Contact page.

Data we receive

The forms on this site store nothing on our servers. The send button opens your email app with a pre-filled message. We only receive the data if you send that email to contribute@dareindo.com yourself.

  • Land reports: the land's location and coordinates, photos, estimated size, condition, ownership status, your description, and your name and phone number if you give them. You may report anonymously.
  • Contributor sign-up: name, email, phone number (optional), place of residence, areas of interest and a short note about you.
  • Contact and partnership messages: name, organisation, topic, message and your email address.
  • Benih waiting list: your email address.

Third-party services

We use no cookies, analytics or advertising. When you open this site, however, your browser connects to services that may log your IP address and technical browser data:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this site.
  • Google Fonts, for typefaces.
  • Google Sheets, from which the Home and Transparency pages read the Public Cash Book figures directly.
  • Our email provider, which receives and stores the emails you send us.

Each service is subject to its own privacy policy.

What we use data for

We use your data only to follow up land reports (including contacting you for a survey), to contact contributors when a suitable activity comes up, to answer messages, and to announce the launch of Benih. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

The legal basis is your consent, which you give by sending us an email. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who we share data with

Only the Dareindo team members handling your message can see it. For a survey we may share the land's location and condition with the landowner, village officials or experts working with us, without the reporter's name or contact details. We share a reporter's name and contact details only with their permission or where the law requires it.

The land map on this site shows only an approximate location and follow-up status, never anyone's personal data.

Public Cash Book

Wages and payments to individuals are recorded by role or group, not by name. Before a transaction receipt is linked publicly, we cover the personal data on it, such as the recipient's name, account number and ID number.

How long we keep data

We keep data as long as needed for the purposes above, and no longer than two years after our last contact with you. Land reports that become project sites are kept while the project runs, but the reporter's name and contact details can still be deleted on request. Financial records are kept as required by tax law.

Your rights

Under the PDP Law you may ask to access and receive a copy of your data, correct inaccurate data, delete your data, withdraw consent, and object to processing. Send requests to contribute@dareindo.com. We will respond within the deadlines set by the PDP Law.

If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you in writing within 3 x 24 hours of becoming aware of it.

Children

The forms on this site are meant for adults. Children under 18 should send information with the consent and help of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the latest version will appear on this page with a new effective date. We will also announce significant changes on the Home page.
