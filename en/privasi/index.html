In short

  • We only ask for the data we need to follow up on land information, contact contributors and prospective partners, and announce the launch of Benih.
  • You can tell us about degraded land without giving your name or contact details.
  • The public land map shows only data about the land, never who told us about it.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.
  • You can ask for a copy, a correction or the deletion of your data at any time at halo@dareindo.com.

This policy is based on Indonesia's Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP). "Personal data" on this page means any data about a person who can be identified, either directly or when combined with other data.

What is made public

Transparency is how we work, but that openness applies to money and impact, not to personal data.

  • The public land map shows the location, condition and follow-up status of verified land. The name and contact details of the person who told us are never shown.
  • Land photos appear only after we strip their metadata and blur any faces and vehicle number plates.
  • The Public Cash Book records wages by role, not by worker's name.
  • Names of partner companies may appear in the Public Cash Book as a source or recipient of funds, unless an agreement says otherwise. Names of individuals are not listed without their consent. [review: policy on listing partner names]

Who receives data

  • Dareindo staff who handle land information, contributors, partnerships or Benih, only as far as their work requires.
  • Field partners, such as surveyors and agroforestry experts who help inspect land. They receive only the data needed for the survey and are bound by confidentiality.
  • Service providers that store or send data on our behalf: Hostinger (email), GitHub (website), Google (Google Sheets for bookkeeping), and a form database provider if we use one later. [update this list if providers change]
  • Government agencies or law enforcement, only where the law requires it.

We do not reveal who sent us land information to the landowner or anyone else without that person's consent.

Your rights

Under UU PDP you have the right to:

  • be told who processes your data, for what purpose and on what basis;
  • access your personal data and receive a copy;
  • complete, update or correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to stop processing, delete or destroy your data;
  • withdraw consent you have given;
  • postpone or restrict the processing of your data;
  • receive your data in a commonly used format and have us send it to another party;
  • sue and receive compensation for unlawful processing of your personal data.

Send your request to halo@dareindo.com with the subject "Personal Data Rights". We may ask you to show that the data is yours before acting on it. We respond within 3 x 24 hours of receiving a request. [review: deadline for each type of right under UU PDP and its implementing regulations]

Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that has already taken place. If you withdraw consent for land information that has already become a project site, we will delete your identity but keep the land data for the project.

If you feel we have not responded properly, you can complain to Indonesia's personal data protection authority. [add the authority's name and contact once it is formally established]

Children

This website is not intended for children under 18. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to send information or messages on your behalf. If we learn that we have received a child's data without a parent's or guardian's consent, we will delete it.
