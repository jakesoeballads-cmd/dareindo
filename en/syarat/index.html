Land information

Not an emergency service

Land information you send is not monitored around the clock and is not an emergency service. If there are signs of a landslide or disaster that threatens people now, contact your local disaster agency (BPBD) or call 112 immediately.

Your responsibilities

  • Describe the land honestly, as far as you know.
  • Send only photos you took yourself or are allowed to share.
  • Do not enter someone else's land without permission, and do not put yourself at risk to take a photo. A photo from a road or public place is enough.
  • Avoid photographing people's faces where you can. If anyone is visible, we will blur them before the photo appears on the public map.

Permission to use your information

By sending land information you give Dareindo a free, non-exclusive permission to use the photos and land data you send to verify and survey the land, show it on the public map, and include it in impact and transparency reports. You keep the copyright in your photos. We do not credit you by name unless you ask us to. [review: licence wording]
